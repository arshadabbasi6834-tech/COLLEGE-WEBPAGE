Our Founder

Shri Shambhu Dayal Ji

The great Vibhuti Karma Yogi, social activist and benefactor, Lala Shambhu Dayal Ji was born on 28 August 1857 into a respected Vaish family. A lifelong follower of simple living and high thinking, he could not remain untouched by the wave of patriotism sweeping India during its struggle for independence.

In 1900 he travelled to Europe, an experience that convinced him education was the true foundation of national progress — for without it, he believed, people could never fully understand their rights and duties. He returned home resolved to establish a school in the city, and Shambhu Dayal College, Ghaziabad, stands today as the living embodiment of his ideals and untiring effort.

Since 1917

SDPG College, Ghaziabad

Shambhu Dayal P.G. College is situated in the city of Ghaziabad, adjoining the border of Delhi. The college began its academic affiliation with the University of Agra from 1962 to 1965, and has been affiliated with C.C.S. University, Meerut, since 1965 — making it one of the most reputed colleges under the university, accredited with a B+ Grade by NAAC.

This great centre of education came into being through the resolve of the late Rai Bahadur Shambhu Dayal Ji, a respected social worker and educationist, who founded a trust for the cause of education by donating his entire estate to it in 1917. The campus stands on the main G.T. Road, about a kilometre from Ghaziabad Railway Station.
